This project was a great lesson in designing around heavy constraints as the existing infrastructure posed many design challenges. Thorough communication and a lot of patience from my supervisor helped everything work out smoothly. By leaning into fast prototyping methods and reviewing many previous iterations, we were able to come out with a clean solution and I ended up with a much better understanding of how to tailor this heavily iterative workflow to a proffesional environment.
